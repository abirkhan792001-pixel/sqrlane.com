Product

Fourteen Workers. One book. Every action gated.

They do not work beside your TMS — they work in it. A booking amendment, an entry, an invoice query, a routing change: each one is a change to a record that came out of the system of record, and each one waits for you.

This page what you get — the roster, the record, the gate

The pitch

Pre-configured agents on the workflows your desk already runs.

Not a generic AI tool you configure yourself. The Workers know what a booking is, what a chokepoint is, and where a decision has to land — because the shape of a forwarder’s day is what they were built against.

01  Purpose-built

An agent for every leg of the shipment lifecycle.

SQRlane is a technology provider for international freight forwarding. The Workers arrive knowing what a booking is, what a chokepoint is, and what a discharge port change breaks — so a disruption arrives, the agent reads it against the book, and the drafts come back before anyone has re-typed the same details into three tools.

Fourteen Workers ship in the roster. Three of them reason live on every run — the tag on each card is the honesty.

02  One book, one platform

Central control over the AI agents that live in your TMS.

The board is read out of the system of record through one connector, and every action a Worker takes goes back onto the record it came from — never a second book to keep. Your central team sees every draft, every queued write, and every rule change on one screen, across every Worker and every lane.

The connector today is a demo connector. The near end is already built; the far end is the integration work behind the trust wall.

03  Plain-language rules

Instructions written like a runbook. Effect on the next run.

The rules a Worker follows are written the way an ops lead would brief a new hire: read this, check that, propose the change, hand it over. Edit a step, save, and the very next trigger runs against the new rule. No deploy, no schema, no ticket to engineering.

Session monitoring records what a human approved, edited or rejected on each draft, so a rule that stops fitting the work is the first thing to be surfaced — not the last.
