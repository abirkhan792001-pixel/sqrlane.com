Part one The everyday desk · nine agents

Your desk, running itself.

Quoting, booking, tracking, documents, invoices, customs — pre-configured against the shape of a forwarder’s day. Each agent reads what came in, drafts what needs to go out, and puts the change on the booking. No second book, no new tool to keep, and no message ever sent without you.

Part two When a lane moves · five agents

The same desk, reacting.

A strike at the port, a river gauge falling, a filing in a customs register, a wildfire across a rail leg. The Watch bucket reads the world across sixty free sources; the Look-ahead bucket sweeps the forward book before the vessel sails; and the everyday agents above pick up what the decision changed — a booking amendment, a customs escalation, an invoice check against a surcharge that was never quoted.
