Six jobs a day. Four disruptions. One desk.

Most of a forwarder’s day is the same six jobs, arriving by mail and ending on a booking. Now and then the world moves a lane and the whole board has to be re-planned. Both run through the same agents, on the same records, and every one of them stops at your approval.

Every state and count above is taken from a real offline run of the same advisor the demo uses — not written by hand. “Handed to the desk” is the work each decision gives the everyday agents: the amendment for the Booking agent, a changed country of entry for Customs, the new ETA for Milestones. The disruptions are authored so one can be shown on command; the reasoning that answers them is not.
